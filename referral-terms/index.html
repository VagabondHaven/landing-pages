Vagabond Haven

Referral programme terms

Version 1.1 Date 5 September 2026 Applies to everyone who joins the referral programme

These are the full terms of the Vagabond Haven referral programme. The short summary in the sign-up form is there for convenience. Where the two differ, this document is the one that applies. It is written in plain language on purpose: if anything here is unclear, ask us before you join.

1

Who this is between

These terms are an agreement between Vagabond Haven AB, a company registered in Sweden, and you, either as a private person or as the business you name when you join. We call you the referral partner.

Joining does not make you an employee, an agent or a representative of Vagabond Haven, and it does not give you authority to enter into anything on our behalf.

2

Joining

You join by completing the sign-up form and confirming that you accept these terms. There is nothing to sign and no fee. We record which version of these terms you accepted and when.

We may decline an application or end a partnership at any time, for example where a partner competes with us directly or where we believe the programme is being misused. Section 9 covers what happens to referrals already registered if that occurs.

3

Your referral link

Once you have joined we give you a personal link and a QR code that points to the same place, plus access to a page where you can see the referrals you have made and the status of each one.

The link is yours to use as often as you like, with no limit on how many people register through it. You may place it anywhere your own audience already finds you, for example your website, your newsletter, your email signature or your premises.

You may not send it as unsolicited marketing to people who have not asked to hear from you, and you may not use it in paid advertising that features the Vagabond Haven name or brand without our written agreement. Both damage our ability to reach customers, and in several countries the first is also unlawful.

4

When a referral counts

A referral is registered when a person submits their own contact details through your link and confirms that we may contact them. We record your name and the date and time against that person.

  • First registration wins. If the same person is later registered by another partner, the first registration is the one that counts.
  • People we already know do not count. If the person is already in our records as a customer or an active enquiry before your registration, no fee is due.
  • A registration is valid for 12 months. If they sign a contract with us within twelve months of registering through your link, your fee is due. After that the registration lapses.
  • One fee per house. Where several partners could claim the same sale, only the first registration is paid.

You can see every referral you have made, and its status, on your own page. If you think something has been attributed wrongly, tell us and we will look at the record together.

5

Your fee

Your fee is 2% of the net sale price of the house, meaning the price we invoice the customer for the house itself, excluding VAT, transport, groundworks and any services carried out by other companies.

  • It becomes due once the customer has paid us in full. Not at order, not at delivery, but at final payment.
  • A ready-built house normally reaches you 2 to 4 weeks after that final payment.
  • A house built to order normally takes 3 to 6 months from contract to final payment, so expect your fee within that period.
  • If a sale falls through, is cancelled or is refunded, no fee is due. If we have already paid you, we will offset it against your next fee, or ask for it back if there is none.
  • There is no cap and no minimum. One referral or a hundred, each qualifying sale is paid the same way.
6

Invoicing and tax

You do not have to invoice us. We prepare the invoice on your behalf and send it to you together with the payment, so there is no paperwork on your side. By joining you agree to us invoicing this way.

If you join as a business, give us your company registration or VAT number so the invoice is correct. You are responsible for your own tax on what you receive, including VAT where it applies to you, and for declaring the income where you live. We cannot advise you on your tax position.

7

What we ask, and what we do

You keep it honest:

  • Only pass your link to people who would genuinely welcome hearing from us.
  • Do not quote prices, delivery dates or specifications, and do not make promises on our behalf. Everything of that kind comes from us, so you are never the one who has to get it right.
  • Use our name and material as we provide them, without altering our logo or claiming to be part of Vagabond Haven.

We do our part:

  • We contact the people you send us once. If the timing is wrong or they are not interested, we thank them and leave them alone. They are not added to any mailing list.
  • We handle the whole conversation, quote, contract, production, delivery and warranty. Nothing is expected of you after the introduction.
  • We keep your referrals and their statuses visible to you, and we pay what is owed without you having to chase it.
8

Personal data

Because people register themselves through your link, you never hand us anybody else's details. From that moment Vagabond Haven is the controller of their data and looks after it under our privacy policy, in the same way as any other customer enquiry.

We hold your own details in order to run the programme and pay you: your name, contact details, company details where relevant, the referrals attributed to you and the record of your acceptance of these terms. You can ask to see what we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted once there is nothing outstanding between us.

Your name is visible to us internally against the people you refer. We do not show it to other partners.

9

Ending your participation

Either of us can end this at any time, with no notice period and no reason needed. You can also simply stop using your link.

Referrals already registered before it ends are still honoured, and are paid in the normal way if they buy within the twelve months in section 4. Your link stops working when the partnership ends.

The exception is misuse. Where a referral was obtained through unsolicited marketing, misrepresentation or false information, no fee is due for it and we may end the partnership immediately.

10

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. The version and date at the top always tell you which one is current, and we will email active partners when something material changes.

A change never affects a referral already registered. Those are settled under the terms in force on the day the referral was made.

11

Law and disagreements

Swedish law applies, and any dispute we cannot settle between us goes to the Swedish courts, with Uppsala District Court as the court of first instance.

Before it ever gets that far, write to us. Almost everything that goes wrong in a referral programme is a question of who registered somebody first, and that is a matter of record rather than of opinion.
