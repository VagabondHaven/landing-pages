Your link does the work

Once you are a partner you never have to write to us again. Everything runs through your own link.

  1. The person you are introducing opens your link or scans your code. It works anywhere your customers already look, from your email signature to a sign at your reception.
  2. They fill in their own contact details. Just a name, an email and a phone number, with no account to create.
  3. They land in our system under your name, with the date and time of that first contact recorded.
  4. You see them on your own page, with the status of each one, from first conversation to delivered house.

Because the person registers themselves, you never have to hand over somebody else's details, and there is no argument later about who introduced whom.

What happens to the people you send us

Your name is attached to every introduction, so here is exactly how we treat them.

We contact them once

If the timing is wrong or they are not interested, we thank them and leave them alone. They are not added to any mailing list and nobody chases them.

You are not expected to sell

No pitching, no follow-up, no answering questions about price, permits or specification. Your link does the introduction and you are done.

Attribution is automatic

Whoever registers through your link is stamped with your name and the time. If the same person turns up later through somebody else, the first registration is the one that counts.

Their details stay with us

We use them only to talk to that person about a tiny house, and we look after them the same way we look after our own customers' data.
